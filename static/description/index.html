Production Planning – Plan Order

Group Sale Orders, forecast demand and customer claims into one plan. Hold stock per BOM level. Produce and buy only what is missing.

Why this module

Built for furniture manufacturers: finished goods are painted / polished / upholstered versions of white (unfinished) components kept in stock. Standard Odoo launches MO / PO the moment a Sale Order is confirmed and has no notion of "this stock is earmarked for that plan". This module adds the planning layer in between.

  • Gate Sale Order confirmation – no OUT / MO / PO until the planner runs a plan.
  • Three demand sources – confirmed Sale Orders, Demand Orders (forecast, make-to-stock) and Customer Claims approved by QA/QC.
  • Hold stock per BOM level – finished goods first, then the shortfall explodes to components, level by level.
  • Net shortfall only – MO / PO are created for what is really missing, using your Inventory routes (Manufacture / Buy, 1-2-3 steps, vendors).

Stock holds per BOM level

Holds are written directly on stock.quant.reserved_quantity using the same core method as stock.move._action_assign. Inventory shows the correct Reserved quantity, other plans see less free stock, but no dummy moves or pickings are created – one call per product instead of one move per line per level.

Planner stays in control

Real-life example

5 finished chairs are in stock and a Sale Order needs 5. The planner knows 2 of them are showroom pieces / promised to another customer / in a colour about to be discontinued. She sets the finished-good hold to 3. The module immediately holds the extra white frames and timber needed to produce the 2 missing chairs.

Later 10 more chairs are received. She raises the finished-good hold back to 5 and the surplus component holds are released for other plans.

  • Edit Quantity on any hold line – decrease releases the difference, increase holds more if available.
  • Release / Hold a whole line.
  • Cascade – changing a hold on level n recomputes every level below it.
  • Recompute lower levels keeps your finished-good decisions and recalculates components; Re-reserve starts from scratch.
  • Priority by line order: upper lines take stock first.

Run Plan

  1. Shortfall is computed from the current holds (what the planner released counts as missing).
  2. Holds are released and, in the same transaction, the plan's own moves reserve the stock – nobody can grab it in between.
  3. Sale Orders and Claims get an OUT delivery (moves are linked to the sale line: delivered quantity and invoicing work as usual). Forecast demand only produces into stock.
  4. One procurement per missing product goes to WH/Stock; Odoo picks Manufacture or Buy from the product routes → MO / PO for the net quantity.
  5. When an MO or a receipt is done, waiting moves of the same plan are reserved automatically.

Setup

  1. Planning ▸ Configuration ▸ Settings: keep Gate SO procurement enabled.
  2. Products: route Manufacture or Buy (up to 19.0; on 20.0 Odoo picks it from the BoM / vendor). Do not use Replenish on Order (MTO) – the module nets the shortfall itself.
  3. Assign users to Planner, Planning Manager, Customer Service (Claim), QA/QC.

Languages: English, Tiếng Việt. Available for Odoo 14.0, 15.0, 16.0, 17.0, 18.0, 19.0 and 20.0 Community — the same unit tests (SO gate, BOM-level hold, cascade, forecast, claim) run green on every version.

Kế hoạch sản xuất – Plan Order (Tiếng Việt)

Gom Sale Order đã xác nhận, Demand Order (dự báo, sản xuất nhập kho) và Claim (CS tạo, QA/QC duyệt) vào một Plan Order. Xác nhận plan → Odoo giữ hàng theo từng BOM level: giữ thành phẩm trước, phần thiếu mới nổ xuống component trắng, rồi nguyên liệu. Chạy plan → phiếu OUT giao khách, MO / PO chỉ cho phần thiếu.

Giữ hàng ghi thẳng lên reserved_quantity của quant (cùng hàm core mà _action_assign dùng) nên Inventory hiện Reserved đúng, không tạo move / phiếu giả, nhanh hơn nhiều so với cách tạo từng move của OCA stock_reserve.

Ví dụ thực tế

Kho có 5 ghế thành phẩm, đơn hàng cần 5. Planner biết 2 cái là hàng showroom / đã hứa cho khách khác / màu cũ sắp đổi → chỉnh giữ còn 3. Module tự giữ thêm khung trắng và gỗ để sản xuất mới 2 cái.

Ngược lại, vừa nhập kho thêm ghế → planner tăng giữ thành phẩm → nhu cầu component giảm → module nhả component ra cho plan khác dùng.

  • Sửa số lượng giữ trực tiếp trên từng dòng, Release / Hold cả dòng.
  • Sửa ở level cha → tự tính lại toàn bộ level con.
  • Tính lại level dưới giữ nguyên quyết định ở thành phẩm; Giữ lại từ đầu làm lại theo tồn kho.
